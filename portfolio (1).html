About me

Creative mind. Marketer's brain.

I'm Menna Allah, a 23-year-old content creator and marketing specialist from Sadat City, Menoufia. I graduated from the Faculty of Tourism & Hotels at Sadat City University, then chose my real passion: digital marketing.

I write scripts and copy that hook from the first second, plan content around real market research, design with Canva, and use AI tools to work faster and smarter. From the strategy to the final reel, I care about every detail — and about the results behind the numbers.

My tourism background taught me how to talk to people and understand what they want. Marketing taught me how to turn that into content that sells.
